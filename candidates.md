# Three candidate identities

Day 2. One paragraph each, each tied to a real condition.

## 1. Jellyfish

**Time:** Present  
**Place:** Underwater, in the ocean  
**Role:** Their bells offer protection for small fish, their movement helps mix water layers, and shifts in their populations can signal changes in ocean health and pollution.

## 2. Pigeon

**Time:** Past  
**Place:** India  
**Role:** Messenger.

## 3. Moon

**Time:** Future  
**Place:** Space, in the solar system  
**Role:** Support life and help maintain and regulate tides.
