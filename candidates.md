# Three candidate identities

Day 2. One paragraph each, each tied to a real condition.

## 1. Shade

**Time:** Present  
**Place:** Everywhere  
**Role:** Follows people and objects, changing in shape and size. Shade represents the parts of ourselves that change depending on where we are and how others see us.

## 2. Seed

**Name:** Origin  
**Time:** Future  
**Place:** Barren land  
**Role:** Carries the possibility of new life. Its future depends on where it lands. Some seeds grow into forests while others remain dormant for years. Origin represents the uncertainty of what something might become.

## 3. Moon

**Name:** Luna  
**Time:** Future  
**Place:** Space  
**Role:** From its surface, Luna watches the planet that once gave it meaning. As humans move towards distant worlds, the Moon becomes a reminder of their origins and their connection to Earth.
